A real pane of glass

Not a blur. A lens.

Every pane is a slab of glass with a rounded rim. Each pixel of the rim is traced through the glass with Snell's law: the backdrop bends around the edges like a shop window, with red, green and blue each bending their own way.

  • The rim lights up on the side facing your pointer.
  • A soft glare follows it across the surface.
  • Press a button: it swells, glows where you touch it, stretches towards your finger and springs back.

Use it

Two lines of setup.

Plain HTML and CSS classes, so it works with any framework. The script adds the refraction and the interactions; without it, you still get good-looking frosted glass. 

import { vitre } from 'vitre';
import 'vitre/style.css';

vitre();
<button class="vitre vitre-button">Save</button>

<div class="vitre vitre-segmented" role="radiogroup">
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="true">Day</button>
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="false">Week</button>
</div>

<label class="vitre-switch"><input type="checkbox"> Wi-Fi</label>
